## Change Communications Plan

For Tallgrass County Schools we ran communications on a
school-year-aware calendar: nothing launched during state testing windows
or the first two weeks of a semester. Each audience — principals, school
secretaries, central office, and payroll staff — had its own message
track, and every communication answered three questions: what changes for
you, when, and who to call. Secretaries got a one-page quick-reference
card per process, laminated and delivered before training, which the
district still reprints. The change workstream sat inside the $1,050,000
two-year program fee.

## Q&A Highlights

Q: How long does hypercare run after go-live?
A: Four weeks per wave as the baseline, extended one week at a time if
ticket volumes stay above the agreed threshold — exit is criteria-based,
not calendar-based.

Q: Will district staff need to be backfilled during the project?
A: Plan for a 0.5 FTE backfill for the payroll lead and business manager
during parallel-testing months; other roles absorb the work within normal
duties. Reference: Aaron Tuck, Assistant Superintendent for Business
Services.
