## Project Management Approach

Cascade Valley Medical Center's replacement program ran on a two-week
sprint cadence inside a stage-gated frame: design, build, test, deploy,
stabilize. The program management office published a single burn-down and
a decision log; every open decision had a named owner and a due date, and
decisions older than ten business days escalated automatically to the
steering committee. Scope change went through a one-page impact template —
schedule, cost, and downstream training impact — before any build work
started. The program delivered on a $2,870,000 fixed-fee basis.

## Governance and Escalation

Governance for Cascade Valley Medical Center had three tiers: a weekly
workstream sync, a biweekly program board with the CIO and CFO, and a
monthly executive steering committee. Risks carried likelihood, impact,
and a mitigation owner; any risk scored red for two consecutive reviews
went to steering with a written mitigation plan. Reference contact: Priya
Raghavan, Chief Information Officer, who co-chaired the program board.
