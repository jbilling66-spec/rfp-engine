## Implementation Methodology

Meridian Health Partners engaged our team to replace a twenty-year-old
general ledger and payroll platform across seven hospitals. We proposed a
two-wave cutover: finance and supply chain in wave one, payroll and human
capital in wave two, with a hard gate between waves requiring three
consecutive clean payroll parallels before wave-two entry. Each wave closed
with a rollback rehearsal, and go/no-go criteria were signed by the
executive sponsor rather than the project team. The total engagement was
delivered for a fixed fee of $1,975,000 across fourteen months.

## Data Migration Approach

Our migration factory ran seven mock conversions before the first
production load for Meridian Health Partners. Legacy chart-of-accounts
segments were mapped through a governed crosswalk workbook with sign-off by
the controller's office, and every mock produced a reconciliation packet:
row counts, hash totals, and out-of-balance exceptions with named owners.
Reference contact for this work: Dana Whitfield, VP of Enterprise
Applications, who chaired the weekly conversion review.
