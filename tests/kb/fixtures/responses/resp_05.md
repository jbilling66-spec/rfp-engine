## Training and Change Management

Our change program paired every department with a trained super-user before
the first classroom session. Training followed a teach-the-workflow model:
role-based curricula built from the client's own configured processes, not
vendor screenshots, delivered in the two weeks before each go-live wave.
Adoption was measured, not assumed — login rates, task completion inside
the new system, and help-desk ticket themes were reviewed weekly for six
weeks after go-live, and departments below threshold received targeted
refresher sessions. Cascade Valley Medical Center's frontline managers
co-taught every session, which doubled voluntary attendance.

## Pricing Narrative

We proposed a fixed fee of $940,000 for the optimization phase, structured
in three milestone payments tied to accepted deliverables rather than
elapsed time. The fee included a capped contingency pool for scope
discovered during design, drawable only with steering-committee approval —
unused contingency returned to Cascade Valley Medical Center at closeout.
