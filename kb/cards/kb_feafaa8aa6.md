The [CLIENT] program ran with a blended team: an onshore
engagement lead, two functional architects for finance and payroll, a
data-conversion lead, and an offshore build pod of six that rotated one
member onshore each quarter for knowledge continuity. Client-side
counterparts were named for every workstream, and no deliverable was
accepted without its counterpart's review. Staffing for the eleven-month
engagement fit within the [FEE] services budget.
