[CLIENT] engaged our team to replace a twenty-year-old
general ledger and payroll platform across seven hospitals. We proposed a
two-wave cutover: finance and supply chain in wave one, payroll and human
capital in wave two, with a hard gate between waves requiring three
consecutive clean payroll parallels before wave-two entry. Each wave closed
with a rollback rehearsal, and go/no-go criteria were signed by the
executive sponsor rather than the project team. The total engagement was
delivered for a fixed fee of [FEE] across fourteen months.
