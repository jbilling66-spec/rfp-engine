For [CLIENT] we ran communications on a
school-year-aware calendar: nothing launched during state testing windows
or the first two weeks of a semester. Each audience — principals, school
secretaries, central office, and payroll staff — had its own message
track, and every communication answered three questions: what changes for
you, when, and who to call. Secretaries got a one-page quick-reference
card per process, laminated and delivered before training, which the
district still reprints. The change workstream sat inside the [FEE]
two-year program fee.
