Hypercare for [CLIENT] ran four weeks per wave with a
dedicated command center: floor-walkers in high-volume departments, a
fifteen-minute triage SLA for payroll-impacting defects, and a daily
stand-down that reviewed every severity-one and severity-two ticket.
Transition to steady-state support required two consecutive weeks under
agreed ticket thresholds, plus a signed knowledge-transfer checklist with
the client's application-support team. The hypercare package was included
in the [FEE] program fee. Reference: [REFERENCE_NAME], VP of Finance
Transformation.
