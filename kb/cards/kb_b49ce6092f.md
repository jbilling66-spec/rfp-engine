[CLIENT] asked for a partner who had moved a K-12 district
off a state-supported legacy finance system without disrupting a single
payroll. Our proposal committed to exactly that: a phased implementation
that kept the legacy system live in parallel until three clean payroll
cycles proved the replacement, staffed by consultants who had done
district conversions in this state and priced at a fixed [FEE] with no
change orders for in-scope work. The board's priorities — payroll
continuity, state reporting compliance, and school-secretary usability —
structured every section that follows.
