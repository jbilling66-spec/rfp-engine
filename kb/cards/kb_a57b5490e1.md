For the utility's biweekly and monthly payroll populations we ran four full
parallel cycles, comparing every earning, deduction, and employer
contribution line against the legacy register at the employee level.
Variances were classified as configuration, data, or legacy-defect, and
only configuration variances blocked exit. The final parallel for [CLIENT] produced 100% net-pay matches across 1,840 employees,
with a signed variance disposition for every prior exception. Union pay
rules — standby, callout, and shift differentials — received dedicated
scenario coverage.
