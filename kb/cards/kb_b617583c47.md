[CLIENT] carried nine years of position-control history and
a chart of accounts shaped by state reporting requirements. Our conversion
approach preserved the state-mandated account-code structure exactly while
remapping local segments, and converted position history in full so
seniority and step-and-lane calculations survived the transition. Three
mock conversions preceded the production load, each reconciled to the
penny against the legacy trial balance and to the position against the
state personnel report.
