We proposed a sixteen-month program for [CLIENT] in
five stages, anchored to the fiscal-year boundary so the first full close
on the new platform aligned with the audit calendar. Key milestones:
design complete at month four, conversion dress rehearsal at month nine,
payroll parallels months ten through twelve, go-live at month thirteen,
and stabilization exit at month sixteen. Each milestone carried written
entry and exit criteria agreed at program start, and the fee schedule of
[FEE] mapped payments to milestone acceptance.
