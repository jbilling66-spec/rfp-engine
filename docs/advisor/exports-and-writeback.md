# Exports and write-back

**Render documents** on the pursuit's **Finish** panel produces them.
Downloads live under two headings and only two: **To the buyer** and
**Internal — do not send**. The buyer copy is the clean response
document; the internal copy carries flags, pending markers, and
findings. The buyer list is read from the submission bundle — the
record of every deliverable produced, refused, or still owed — never
from what happens to sit in a folder, and filled buyer forms list
there beside the response document.

The submission export refuses while packaging is blocked or a section
still owes content to an unanswered gap — resolve or waive first.

Write-back fills the buyer's own workbook: **Preview write-back** shows
exactly which cells will be written and which are refused (pricing grids
and non-prose cells are never written by the engine; unplanned cells are
refused; undrafted cells stay empty for you). **Confirm write-back**
writes a COPY under the internal downloads — the uploaded original is
never modified. On a firm-template response, **Complete by hand** takes
the values only a person supplies; **Save values** records them for the
next write-back.
