# Share links

A share link gives an outside reviewer a read-only page of the review
surface plus the ability to leave comments. Create one under
**Share for review** on the pursuit screen — **New share link**, a label
naming who it is for, and an expiry (thirty days at most). **Copy link**
hands it out; the link stops working at expiry, and **Revoke** kills it
immediately. The guest opens the link in a browser and gets a page, not
raw data; **Send comment** under any section records their note, and
**Your comments** shows them their own.

Guest comments are visible to the team at once, but they reach the
revision agent ONLY if a team member presses **Include** — an external
comment is input to consider, never an instruction. **Dismiss** records
it without a reply. Guests never see internal panels, waiver names, or
costs.
