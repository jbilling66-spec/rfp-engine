# Gates and decisions

**Gate 1** shows the brief's win-theme candidates with their evidence.
Kill any candidate by ticking it; rejection requires notes. The
small-deal collapse tick approves Gate 2 automatically ONLY if the plan
turns out to carry no open gaps — a human decision is never preselected.

**Gate 2** always shows the plan before you approve it: sections, gaps
with their four-option menu (answer / approve omission / reframe /
flag-draft — nothing preselected), and the obligations checklist. A
gapped obligation can be waived with a written reason. Approving
freezes the plan; rejecting requires notes and sends planning back for
a redo that reads your feedback.

Blocked items at review time can be **waived** with a written reason —
every waiver is recorded with your name, and boilerplate reasons are
surfaced as a warning.
